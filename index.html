02 / Sportsbook

India’s first fixed-odds portal for horse racing.

Partnered with Razor Gaming Private Limited, a part of Hyderabad Race Club, one of India’s premier racecourses, to develop a comprehensive solution for all sports booking needs within the horse racing industry. This collaboration has led to the creation of India’s first fixed odds portal in horse racing.

The platform offers a multi-feed system, seamlessly aggregating domestic, international, and virtual racing events in one location, simplifying the online racing experience for users.

Additionally, Terminal / Mobile PoS solution provides a flexible and intuitive point-of-sale system for efficient transaction processing and payments, ensuring a smooth, user-friendly experience.

  • Fixed odds
  • Domestic, international & virtual feeds
  • Terminal & mobile PoS
